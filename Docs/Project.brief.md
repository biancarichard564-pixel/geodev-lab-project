# My Project brief

## The question 
What is the proportion of dry land relative to open water bodies and wetlands across  Burutu Local Government Area ,Delta state ?

## Why it matters 
Urban Planning & Estate Development: Limited dry land means projects require careful surveying, reclamation, and drainage design to avoid flooding.
Agriculture & Livelihoods: Fishing and aquaculture thrive in wetlands, while farming is constrained to small dry patches.
Environmental Protection: Wetlands are crucial for biodiversity and carbon storage; reclaiming them for estates can worsen flood risk and erode ecosystems

## The data I need
Nigeria state Bounaries

## what i will build 
A land-use map that updates monthly, showing the proportion of dry land, open water, and wetlands across Burutu. This would help planners visualize changes in real time.

An interactive dashboard that local government officers or estate developers could open to see where dry land is most stable, where wetlands are shrinking, and where water bodies pose flood risks.

A risk-alert service that flags communities at high risk of flooding after heavy rainfall, based on the balance of wetlands and waterlogged terrain.
